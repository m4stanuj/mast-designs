About the Architect

The mind
behind M4ST

I am Anuj — a solo AI systems architect operating from Bareilly, India. I build autonomous, multi-agent AI infrastructures that think independently, collaborate across workflows, and execute without human hand-holding.

My philosophy is radical: maximum capability at zero recurring cost. The M4ST stack runs 21 MCP servers across 11 free LLM providers with ChromaDB-powered semantic memory — entirely local-first, entirely offline-capable.

I am available for exclusive AI engineering engagements — from architectural consulting to full-scale autonomous pipeline builds. If you need AI that genuinely works without a cloud bill, you've found the right architect.
